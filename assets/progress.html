Practice progress

Learn in any order and practise as often as you like. Reading and Listening scores are saved when you check answers; Writing drafts and Speaking sessions are logged when you submit them. You never tick a completion box or enter a score yourself.

Your learning activity

A higher score on the same questions may reflect remembering answers, not a higher IELTS band. Writing length and Speaking duration show practice, not quality. Your teacher can review each draft or session on a simple 1–5 feedback scale (not an IELTS band).

Reading & Listening practice over time

Open any Reading module's final practice slide and check all answers; or use Check My Answers in Listening. Each checked attempt is saved with its date and score. Repeat an activity to see the trend, but remember that the questions repeat.

Writing drafts

Use the response box on the final practice slide in Academic Writing Task 1 or Task 2. Each submission is kept as a separate draft. A teacher can read it and rate development—word count alone does not measure improvement.

Speaking practice

Choose the part you want to practise. Allow microphone access, speak, then stop. The recording uploads privately so the owner and an authorised school teacher can play it and give feedback. This free-tier pilot limits each recording to 5 MB and keeps audio for up to 30 days; some browsers may generate a full mock that is too large.

Teacher feedback and improvement

Teacher reviews are linked to a specific Writing draft or Speaking session. Ratings are a simple teaching scale from 1 (needs work) to 5 (strong), not IELTS band scores.

Older records and privacy

Older planning records and manually entered results remain in the database for continuity but do not appear in this learning view. Practice entries created by the previous version have been carried over where possible. Earlier overwritten Writing drafts cannot be recovered. Teachers see only their current school's students; the owner sees all non-owner members. Writing text and feedback are stored in D1. New Speaking audio is private and stops being playable after 30 days; expired audio is physically deleted at the next recording upload, or the owner can delete it sooner. Older metadata-only sessions have no audio.
